# User guide (plain English)

**What it does.** You pick a place in Nigeria. TerraSense shows which published, citable datasets
have something to say about that place, what they say, when the data were observed, and how detailed
they are.

**What it does not do.** It cannot tell you whether a road, field or site is safe to drive on or
build on, and it never will. It has no live flood information.

**How to use it**

1. Click the map, type a latitude/longitude, or press a preset.
2. Optionally pick a date. The app will remind you that its layers come from other dates.
3. Read the **Status**:
   - ⬜ **Abstained**: there isn't enough evidence to say anything about ground conditions.
   - 🟥 **Observed environmental hazard**: a dataset recorded flooding or water here in the past.
     This is a warning, not a forecast. Never drive into floodwater.
   - 🟨 **Preliminary**: some measured evidence exists, but an engineer must review it.
     (This status cannot occur yet, because no measured soil data is available.)
4. The tabs show each dataset's reading, observation period and resolution, with notes on its limits.
5. Use **Export** to save a JSON or CSV record of exactly what was shown.

**Words to watch**

- "Bare soil" on a map means *uncovered ground*, not sand.
- A **drainage basin** is the area that drains to a river. It is not a wetland.
- **Historical** means "as observed then", not "as it is today".

Whatever the app shows, have a qualified engineer inspect the site before using vehicles or equipment there.
